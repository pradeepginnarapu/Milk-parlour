## Our story

Sravanthi Milk Parlour began with a simple idea: every household in Rangashipet deserves fresh, pure and affordable dairy. From daily milk to curd, paneer, butter and ghee, we stock the products families rely on, and handle them with care.

Today, we are proud to be a friendly, familiar name for neighbours across Warangal.
